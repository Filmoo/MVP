A League companion that shows its work.

MVP is a small, free app for Windows that follows your League client from the tray: picks for your role in champion select, each with its reasons; your build in one click; the players of your game on the loading screen; a grade for every game, with what earned it.

Download for Windows See what’s new

no account · no ads · no tracking

What it does

  • Champion select

    Picks for your role, starting from the champions you play, ranked with the numbers behind them. Statistics only: nothing is picked for you.

  • Builds in one click

    Runes, item set and summoner spells written into your client, in one click or on lock-in if you turn that on. Your own pages and sets are never changed.

  • Loading screen

    Rank, most played champions and recent results of every player the game shows. Players in streamer mode stay hidden.

  • Profiles and grades

    Search any Riot ID. Every game of a match history gets a grade from S+ to C, with the two or three facts behind it.

  • Tier list and champions

    Ranked and ARAM statistics from Emerald up, per patch: tiers, builds, runes, skill order and matchups.

  • ARAM

    Your champion and the bench ranked by your team’s chances, with their reasons. ARAM builds serve ARAM: Mayhem games too.

  • Glass, in two languages

    An interface of liquid glass that bends the light behind it, in English and in French.

  • Quiet in the tray

    MVP keeps following the client with its window closed. Auto-accept is there if you want it, off by default.

What it won’t do

  • Name players the game hides: your ranked teammates in champion select, anyone in streamer mode.
  • Time enemy cooldowns, estimate MMR or tell you to dodge.
  • Run inside your game: no overlay, nothing injected.
  • Ask for an account, show ads or track you.

Open source

MVP is free software: its code is public on GitHub under the GNU AGPL-3.0, so you can read it, build it and change it. Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue.
